Our Story

Chain & Bracelet started on one bench with one idea: jewellery you put on once and stop thinking about. Everything we make still passes through the same hands it always has.

From a Single
Bench

We started small — a single bench, a handful of tools, and a conviction that fine jewellery didn't need to mean precious-once-a-year. Every chain and bracelet we set is still made the same way it was on day one: to order, in solid gold, by the same small team that answers your emails.

No plating, no filler metals, no stones we can't trace. If a piece isn't good enough to wear every day for years, we don't ship it.

How It's Made

01

Sketch

Every piece starts as a hand drawing, sized and weighted to the request before any metal is touched.

02

Cast

Cast in-house in solid gold — 10K through 18K, never plated — straight from the sketch.

03

Set

Stones are set by hand, one at a time under magnification, and checked twice before they leave the bench.

04

Polish

Polished, inspected and boxed in our studio the same week — nothing sits in a warehouse first.

Solid Gold, Always

10K through 18K, never plated. What it's stamped is what's under the finish, for the life of the piece.

Responsibly Sourced

Lab-grown and natural stones, each graded for cut before colour, with a certificate you can trace back.

Made to Order

Nothing sits in a warehouse. Every piece is set in our studio the week you order it, by hand.

Questions

How do I find my ring size?

Use our free printable sizer, or measure a ring you already own — the full guide is linked from every ring's product page.

Do you offer engraving?

Yes — hand engraving is included at no extra charge on any chain, bracelet or band.

How long does an order take?

Everything is made to order in our studio, typically eight to ten working days, then shipped insured.

What's your return policy?

Thirty days, insured both ways. Resizing within one size is free for the first year.

Is the gold solid or plated?

Always solid — 10K through 18K — never plated. What it's stamped is what's under the finish.

Can I layer multiple pieces?

Yes, most of what we make is designed to be layered. See the length guide on any chain or bracelet for spacing tips.

Still Have Questions?

Ring sizing, chain length, engraving, or just deciding between two pieces — we're a small team and we read every message ourselves.
