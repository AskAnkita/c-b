    Measure a bracelet you already own and like the fit of, laid flat end to end — or wrap a strip of paper snug around the wrist and measure that.

    • 6.5 inSnug — sits close to the wrist
    • 7 inStandard — the most common fit
    • 7.5 inLoose — worn low on the wrist

    Between sizes? Most people size up for a bracelet they'll layer with others.

    Chain length is measured end to end around the neck. If you have a chain you like the fall of, lay it flat and measure it.

    • 16 inChoker — sits at the base of the throat
    • 18 inCollarbone — our most popular length
    • 20 inJust below the collarbone

    Layering two or three? Space each length about two inches apart so they don't tangle.
